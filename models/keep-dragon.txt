keep-dragon.glb: Keepfall's dragon (owner-only art, Evan's profile only; keep it out of sw.js precache)

Credit (CC-BY-4.0, show wherever the dragon is credited):
This work is based on "Mountain Dragon" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/mountain-dragon-0a0665396f634b0e9d0872720087f52c) by Alexey Zaika (https://sketchfab.com/lexazaixa25) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Changes from the original: reduced to five clips cut from the single 96.77 s "Take 001" reel (two of them derived, see below),
re-scaled and re-oriented, the two duplicate-vertex meshes merged into one primitive, textures shrunk to 1024 px JPEG,
and the KHR_materials_clearcoat layer dropped.

Model (2.22 MB GLB, three.js GLTFLoader, no extensions):
- 1 skinned mesh "DragonMesh" (12,058 vertices, 20,460 triangles), 96 joints, 1 material (MeshStandardMaterial, double-sided):
  baseColor, normal, occlusion+roughness+metallic packed in one texture (R/G/B), and emissive (glowing eyes and mouth), each 1024 px.
- Units: 1 unit = nose to tail of the outstretched glide pose (source 23.0 s spans z -0.5..+0.5). Wingspan about 1.48.
  The dragon faces +Z with +Y up, so its left wing is +X. y = 0 is the ground under its feet in the standing idle.
  x = 0 is its centre line. The scene root is "KeepDragon".
- Joint names have been cleaned up: COG (hips/root motion), Pelvis, Spine, Spine1, Neck..Neck3, Head, WingL_/WingR_*, L_/R_ legs,
  Tail..Tail10. Use Head as the origin for fire.
- Original bounds: mesh POSITION x -946.75..946.75, y -1018.70..548.76, z -869.07..65.26 (source units, vertical bind pose).
  Sketchfab scene about 2185 x 1112 x 2072. Animated glide pose: 1544.43 long, wingspan 2285. The source faced yaw 16.8 deg,
  and its ground was at y = -319.89. Scale is 1/1544.43 = 0.00064749.

Clips (all sampled at the reel's own 30 fps, with redundant keys dropped; only rotations plus COG/Spine1 translation are animated):
  fly     0.800 s  loop  Derived: the hover loop (29.167-29.967 s) with the COG pitched 30 deg nose-down and the neck lifted
                         14 deg, so the body is near-level, wings flap and legs and tail trail behind. The reel has no
                         forward-flapping cycle, only a glide pose and the upright hover. Seamless.
  hover   0.800 s  loop  29.167-29.967 s (frames 875-899), the upright flapping hover. The last 0.2 s are cross-blended with
                         28.967-29.167 s so the wrap is seamless in pose and speed. COG bobs between y 0.13 and 0.22.
  attack  0.833 s  once  32.000-32.833 s (frames 960-985), an aerial strike from the hover. It rears with the head high
                         (0-0.5 s), lunges the head forward and down (0.53-0.75 s; fire or bite at about 0.6-0.7 s), then
                         returns to the hover base pose. It starts abruptly, so cross-fade into it over about 0.15 s.
  hit     0.800 s  once  Derived: the recoil that opens the in-air death (41.000-41.350 s), then eased back by 0.8 s to the
                         hover base pose (32.000 s). The reel has no in-air hit or flinch, so drop this clip if it reads wrong.
  death   3.600 s  once  41.000-44.600 s (frames 1230-1338), shot down. Recoil (0-0.6 s), tip over head-first (0.6-1.3 s),
                         hang head-down with wings folded IN PLACE (1.3-3.0 s, COG y about 0.48). The impact at 3.0-3.2 s
                         drops the COG to about 0.08, and it lies on the ground until 3.6 s. Play it with clampWhenFinished,
                         and move the dragon down during 1.3-3.0 s so it lands at the impact.

Skinned bounding spheres are computed from the first pose. Set frustumCulled = false or give the mesh a generous sphere.
For several dragons, use SkeletonUtils.clone(gltf.scene) with one AnimationMixer each (tested).
